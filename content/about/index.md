# About

**Hi! Olá! 你好! Apa khabar?** I'm Ling Ling.

I'm a Doctoral Researcher and Intercultural Psychologist. Essentially, I conduct research, write papers, give talks, and facilitate workshops. My research is grounded in social psychology, with specific interests in intercultural relations, migration, and the asylum-seeker and refugee population.

My PhD research focuses on **asylum-seekers and refugees' psychological and sociocultural adaptation** in resettled and transit countries. I use a participatory approach in my research to generate new knowledge that informs researchers and practitioners in their programmes and interventions.

I am also a member of:
- The Global Network of Psychologists for Human Rights (GNPHR)
- The International Association for Cross-Cultural Psychology (IACCP)
- The International Association for Intercultural Relations (IAIR)
- The International Council of Psychologists (ICP)

If you're interested in collaborating on research, or discussing cross-cultural adaptation, [please get in touch](mailto:linglingtai@pm.me).
